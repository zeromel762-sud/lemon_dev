About Japan

Japan (日本, Nihon or Nippon) is an island nation in East Asia made up of four main islands — Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku, and Kyushu — plus thousands of smaller ones stretching from subtropical Okinawa in the south to the cool north of Hokkaido.

Japanese is written with three scripts working together: hiragana and katakana, each a complete phonetic alphabet, and kanji, characters borrowed and adapted from Chinese centuries ago. Tenarai starts you on all three, one tab at a time.

02 · Writing

Trace it on the manuscript grid

Genkōyōshi — the squared paper Japanese students have used for a century — keeps every character the same size. Trace the faint guide, then draw it again from memory below.

a
How to trace well: keep strokes in the same order every time — top to bottom, left to right, and horizontal before vertical when the two cross. Order matters more than speed; it's what makes the character legible when written quickly later.

Checking your writing: press ✓ check my writing and the app compares your ink against the character's shape and position on the grid. Get a close enough match and next → unlocks; otherwise clear and try again. It's a shape check, not full handwriting recognition — use your own judgment too.

03 · Speaking

Listen, then say it back

Press listen to hear each phrase at a natural pace, then press the microphone and repeat it. Your browser checks what it heard against the phrase — a loose match is enough.

Speech recognition isn't available in this browser/embed. You can still use Listen to hear correct pronunciation — open this page in a standalone browser tab (Chrome desktop or Android) to unlock the microphone practice.

04 · Translate

Speak one language, hear the other

Choose which language you're about to speak, press the mic, and say a word or sentence. It's recognized, translated, and spoken back automatically — English in becomes spoken Japanese, and Japanese in becomes spoken English. This needs an internet connection for the translation step; everything else on this page still works offline.
