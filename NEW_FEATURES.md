If you have a **Fish** pet, you can now buy a **Fish Bowl** for it!

- 🫙 **Fish Bowl** - 150 🪙

- Displayed in **Pet Accessories** section

1. Buy **Fish** pet (800 🪙)
2. Summon fish as active pet
